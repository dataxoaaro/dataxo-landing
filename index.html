Most AI projects die at the demo.

We build the ones that survive production. LLM agents wired into real systems, with evals, guardrails, and a team that owns them after we leave.

The problem

The demo takes two weeks. It works on the happy path, in a notebook, on your best data. Everyone is impressed.

Then comes everything else. Edge cases. Eval suites. Cost per run. Latency budgets. Model drift. The gap between a demo and a dependable system is where most AI budgets disappear.

That gap is our entire business.
